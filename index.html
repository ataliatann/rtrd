How it works

A simple way to experience Singapore on the run

You don't have to be a fast or experienced runner. We run at a relaxed, conversational pace, with stops along the way for stories, photos and the best views. Groups are kept small, and no one gets left behind.

1

Select your tour

Choose from three distinct running experiences, ranging from 4 to 8 km. Each route offers a different perspective of Singapore, from iconic landmarks to historic neighbourhoods.

2

Make a booking

Select your preferred tour date and indicate the number of participants. Once your booking is confirmed, you will receive the meeting point and essential tour information.

3

Meet your guide

Arrive at the designated meeting point. Your licensed tourist guide will lead the route, set a comfortable group pace and provide commentary throughout the experience.

About

Meet Atalia

Hi, I’m Atalia! If it involves running, travelling, adventure or trying something new, I’m probably in. I’ve been running since my school days, and I’ve always had an adventurous individual. Whether it’s exploring a new country, riding my motorbike around Singapore or saying yes to something new, I’ve always been the first to sign up. Running has become one of my favourite ways to discover a place. That’s how Run the Red Dot started. I wanted to share that experience with travellers, combining running, adventure and the stories of the city I call home. My runs are relaxed and easy-going. We’ll run, chat, stop for stories and photos, and explore Singapore together.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need to be a runner?

No. The pace is relaxed and beginner friendly. You run in short sections and stop often for stories, photos and rest.

How big are the groups?

Small. Each run takes up to 8 people so there is time for everyone.

What happens if it rains?

The route may be adjusted, shortened, delayed or cancelled because of weather, public events, closures, crowd conditions or safety concerns. We will be in touch by WhatsApp if anything changes.

How do I choose a date?

You put your preferred date in the registration form. We will confirm the date and the exact meeting spot with you by WhatsApp.

What should I bring?

Running shoes, water, comfortable clothes.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund if booking is cancelled 24h before start of tour.

Can you run a private group?

Yes, drop us a message and we will get in touch.

Is there an age limit? Can I bring children?

As long as your child can run the selected distance.
