How it works

Turn up, run a bit, learn a lot

Every run is capped at 8 people so it stays personal. You run in easy sections, then stop for a story, a photo or a breather, then carry on. No one gets left behind and you do not need to be a runner to enjoy it.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need to be a runner?

No. The pace is relaxed and beginner friendly. You run in short sections and stop often for stories, photos and rest. If you can walk briskly for a few kilometres you will be fine. Tell Atalia at registration if you have any concerns.

How big are the groups?

Small. Each run takes up to 8 people so there is time for everyone.

What happens if it rains?

The route may be adjusted, shortened, delayed or cancelled because of weather, public events, closures, crowd conditions or safety concerns. Atalia will be in touch by WhatsApp if anything changes.

How do I choose a date?

You put your preferred date in the registration form. Atalia will confirm the date and the exact meeting spot with you by WhatsApp.
